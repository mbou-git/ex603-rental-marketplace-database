# To the Leadership Team

The platform-wide failure rate — viewings that ended cancelled or disputed — is 22%: 44 out of 200. That's the real number behind the follow-up question from last quarter.

It's not even across properties. Looking only at properties with enough viewings for their rate to mean anything (12 properties with 10 or more viewings each), the typical failure rate among them is about 24%. Three sit well above that: property 3 (38.5%, 5 of 13 viewings), property 1 (36.4%, 4 of 11), and property 9 (35.7%, 5 of 14) — each roughly 12 to 15 points above what's normal for a property with enough data to judge. I'd start an investigation with those three.

All three are near the low end of what counts as "enough data" (11 to 14 viewings each), so I'd treat these rates as a strong signal to look into, not a final verdict — a couple more viewings either way could move the number meaningfully. For contrast, property 10 has 19 viewings and zero failures; worth understanding what it's doing differently, if anything there is repeatable.

It varies by region too, separately from the property-level picture above. Hue has the highest failure rate at 31% (11 of 36), Hanoi the lowest at 8% (3 of 36) — close to a four-to-one spread between the best and worst city. Haiphong (28%) and Danang (25%) sit close to the platform average; Saigon is a bit under at 19%.
